Contact

Questions, bugs or a calculator you'd like added: email dalynsnyder@hotmail.com. Include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

How does the free trial work?

Everything is free for 14 days. After that, a one-time $9.99 unlock keeps Rig Buddy working for good. There's no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.

I bought it already. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android), open Rig Buddy and tap Restore purchase.

How do I move my jobs to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings › Backup & Restore and tap Export Backup File. Send the file to yourself, then tap Import Backup File on the new phone. Your jobs live only on your phone, so back up before you switch.

Where do saved PDFs go?

iPhone: the Files app under On My iPhone › Rig Buddy. Android: Documents › RigBuddy.

Does it need signal?

No. Every calculator works offline. Oil prices and rig counts update when you have signal and keep the last numbers when you don't.
